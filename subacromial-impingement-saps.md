# Subacromial Impingement / Pain Syndrome (SAPS)

## What is Subacromial Impingement Syndrome?

It's when structures in the shoulder (like tendons or bursa) get pinched under the acromion when you lift your arm—especially overhead.

**This causes:**
- Pain with overhead movement
- Weakness
- Limited motion

---

## The 4 Biomechanical Problems (Conditions)

### 1) Scapular Dyskinesia

**What it is:** The shoulder blade (scapula) is not moving correctly.

**Normal movement:**
- After ~30° of arm lift
- 2:1 ratio → for every 2° the arm (humerus) moves, the scapula moves 1°
- The scapula should upwardly rotate when lifting the arm

**Problem:**
- Scapula doesn't rotate upward enough
- Leads to pinching under the acromion

**Exercises to fix it:**
- Scapular retraction (pinching shoulder blades together)
- Wall slides
- Serratus anterior strengthening (e.g., push-up plus)
- Lower trap strengthening (Y raises)

---

### 2) Glenohumeral Joint Instability

**What it is:** The shoulder joint is too loose (lax ligaments) + weak rotator cuff

**Problem:**
- Humeral head moves too much
- It rides upward → causes impingement

**Exercises to fix it:**
- Rotator cuff strengthening:
  - External rotation with band
  - Internal rotation with band
- Isometric shoulder stability exercises
- Closed-chain exercises (like wall push-ups)

---

### 3) Poor Posture

**What it is:**
- Rounded shoulders
- Forward head
- Thoracic kyphosis (hunched upper back)

**Problem:**
- Narrows space under acromion
- Puts shoulder in bad position → easier to impinge

**Exercises to fix it:**
- Postural correction:
  - Chin tucks
  - Thoracic extension exercises
- Stretch tight muscles:
  - Chest (pectoralis stretch)
- Strengthen weak muscles:
  - Mid/lower traps
  - Rhomboids

---

### 4) Tight Posterior Capsule

**What it is:** The back part of the shoulder capsule is tight

**Problem:**
- Pushes humeral head forward/upward
- Leads to impingement during movement

**Exercises to fix it:**
- Posterior capsule stretches:
  - Cross-body stretch
  - Sleeper stretch
- Gentle joint mobility exercises

---

## Easy Way to Remember

Think:
- Scapula not moving right → fix control
- Joint too loose → strengthen stability
- Posture bad → open up chest, strengthen back
- Capsule tight → stretch it

---

## Super Simple Summary

Subacromial impingement happens when the shoulder gets pinched during movement because of:
1. Bad scapula movement
2. Weak/unstable shoulder joint
3. Poor posture
4. Tight back capsule

**Treatment** = strengthen what's weak + stretch what's tight + fix movement
