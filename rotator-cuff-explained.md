# Rotator Cuff Muscles ("SITS" Muscles)

| Muscle | Origin | Insertion | Function |
|--------|--------|-----------|----------|
| **Supraspinatus** | Supraspinous fossa of scapula | Greater tuberosity (top) | Initiates abduction |
| **Infraspinatus** | Infraspinous fossa of scapula | Greater tuberosity (back) | External rotation |
| **Teres Minor** | Lateral border of scapula | Greater tuberosity (bottom) | External rotation |
| **Subscapularis** | Subscapular fossa | Lesser tuberosity | Internal rotation |

> **All insert on the humerus** - they stabilize the Glenohumeral joint.

---

# How These Fit Together

## Separate Joints = Separate Muscle Groups

**Scapular muscles** → move the scapula (on the rib cage)
**Glenohumeral muscles** → move the arm (on the scapula)
**Rotator cuff** → stabilizes the shoulder joint

---

## Your Muscles Explained

| Muscle | Joint(s) Involved | What It Does |
|--------|-------------------|--------------|
| **Latissimus Dorsi** | Shoulder (GH) | Extension, adduction, internal rotation |
| **Pectoralis Major** | Shoulder (GH) | Flexion, adduction, internal rotation |
| **Pectoralis Minor** | Scapula | Depression, protraction, downward rotation |
| **Deltoids** (Ant/Middle/Post) | Shoulder (GH) | Flexion (ant), Abduction (middle), Extension (post) |
| **Sartorius** | Hip & Knee | Hip flexion, abduction, external rotation (NOT a shoulder muscle!) |
| **Serratus Anterior** | Scapula | Protraction, upward rotation, holds scapula to ribs |

---

## Serratus Anterior (the "Boxer's Muscle")

- **Origin:** Lateral surface of ribs 1-8 (or 9)
- **Insertion:** Medial border of scapula (anterior surface)
- **Actions:** 
  - Protraction (scapular abduction)
  - Upward rotation
  - Holds scapula against rib cage

> Think "punching" — Serratus Anterior protracts the scapula when you punch.

---

# Quick Way to Think of It

**Shoulder complex has 2 joints:**
1. **Scapulothoracic** (scapula on ribs) → scapular muscles
2. **Glenohumeral** (arm on scapula) → deltoids, pecs, lats + rotator cuff stabilizes it

**Rotator cuff = stabilizers** — they hold the humeral head in the socket
**Deltoids/Pecs/Lats = movers** — they create the big arm movements

The sartorius is a LEG muscle — it crosses the hip! Don't worry about it for shoulder/scrapular motions.
