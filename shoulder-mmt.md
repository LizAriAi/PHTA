# Shoulder MMT Prime Movers & Testing Positions

## Flexion
- **Prime movers:** Anterior Deltoid, Pectoralis Major
- **Gravity required:** Sitting, lift arm up into flexion
- **Gravity eliminated:** Side-lying

## Extension
- **Prime movers:** Latissimus Dorsi, Posterior Deltoid, Teres Major
- **Gravity required:** Prone
- **Gravity eliminated:** Side-lying

## Internal Rotation
- **Prime movers:** Subscapularis, Pec Major, Latissimus Dorsi, Anterior Deltoid, Teres Major
- **Gravity required:** Prone, arm at 90° abduction, towel under arm, elbow hanging off table at 90°
- **Gravity eliminated:** Sitting, PTA supports at wrist and elbow

## External Rotation
- **Prime movers:** Infraspinatus, Teres Minor, Posterior Deltoid
- **Gravity required:** Prone, arm at 90° abduction, towel under arm, elbow hanging off table at 90°
- **Gravity eliminated:** Sitting, PTA supports at wrist and elbow

## Adduction
- **Prime movers:** Latissimus Dorsi, Pectoralis Major, Teres Major
- **Gravity required:** Side-lying on testing arm, lift up/adduct across body
- **Gravity eliminated:** Sitting
