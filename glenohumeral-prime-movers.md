# Glenohumeral Joint (Shoulder) - Prime Movers

| Motion | Prime Mover |
|--------|-------------|
| **Flexion** | Anterior Deltoid, Pectoralis Major (clavicular head) |
| **Extension** | Posterior Deltoid, Latissimus Dorsi |
| **Abduction** | Middle Deltoid, Supraspinatus |
| **Adduction** | Pectoralis Major, Latissimus Dorsi |
| **Internal Rotation** | Subscapularis |
| **External Rotation** | Infraspinatus, Teres Minor |

> **Note:** Prime mover = the muscle primarily responsible for the movement
