# Scapulohumeral Rhythm

## The 2:1 Ratio

- **Total shoulder flexion/abduction:** 180°
- **Glenohumeral joint:** 120°
- **Scapulothoracic (scapula):** 60°

## Scapula Movement Pattern

- Moves the **whole time** during arm elevation
- **Minimal at first** (0–30°) — scapulohumeral rhythm starts gradually
- **More obvious after 30°** — scapula starts rotating significantly

## WHY This Matters

- Prevents impingement
- Keeps joint stable
- Allows full ROM

---

# Force Couples

## Upward Rotation Force Couple

**Purpose:** Move scapula upward (lifting arm overhead)

**Muscles:**
- Upper trapezius → pulls up
- Lower trapezius → pulls down
- Serratus anterior → pulls forward

**WHY it works:** Together they produce smooth upward rotation — Upper Trap + Lower Trap pull opposite directions but both contribute to upward rotation when Serratus Anterior adds the forward pull.

---

## Glenohumeral Joint Stability Force Couple

**Purpose:** Keep humeral head centered in glenoid fossa

**Muscles:**
- Deltoid (especially middle fibers) → pulls humerus **up**
- Rotator cuff → pulls humerus **down** + stabilizes
  - Supraspinatus
  - Infraspinatus
  - Teres minor
  - Subscapularis

**WHY:** Deltoid pulls superiorly (causes impingement at acromion), rotator cuff counters this by pulling inferiorly. Prevents humeral head from translating upward.

# Ligaments (Basic Function)

## Glenohumeral (GH) Joint

| Ligament | Function |
|----------|----------|
| **Superior GH** | Limits inferior translation |
| **Middle GH** | Limits anterior movement |
| **Inferior GH** ⭐ | MOST IMPORTANT — stabilizes in abduction + ER (dislocation position) |

## Elbow Joint

| Ligament | Function |
|----------|----------|
| **Ulnar Collateral (UCL)** | Resists valgus stress (medial elbow) |
| **Radial Collateral (RCL)** | Resists varus stress (lateral elbow) |

## Wrist Joint

| Ligaments | Function |
|-----------|----------|
| **Palmar ligaments** | Stronger, stabilize wrist in flexion |
| **Dorsal ligaments** | Weaker |

**WHY:** Wrist more stable in flexion than extension (anatomical position favors flexion stability).

# Forearm Motions & Prime Movers

## Pronation

- **Prime movers:** Pronator Teres, Pronator Quadratus

**WHY:** Both cross anteriorly → pull radius over ulna (forearm turns palm down)

## Supination

- **Prime movers:** Biceps Brachii ⭐ (strongest), Supinator

**WHY:** Both wrap around radius → rotate it back to anatomical position (forearm turns palm up)

---

# Elbow MMT

## Elbow Flexion
- **Position:** Test at ~90° flexion
- **Resistance:** Resist further flexion

## Elbow Extension
- **Position:** Start flexed
- **Resistance:** Resist extension

## Gravity Minimized
- Perform in **horizontal plane** (sitting, arm supported on table)

---

# Power vs Precision Grips

## Power Grip
- Whole hand involved
- Example: holding a hammer

## Precision Grip
- Fingers only (fine motor control)
- Example: holding a pencil

# Key Muscle Attachments

## Rotator Cuff (SITS)

| Muscle | Origin | Insertion |
|--------|--------|-----------|
| **Supraspinatus** | Supraspinous fossa | Greater tubercle |
| **Infraspinatus** | Infraspinous fossa | Greater tubercle |
| **Teres Minor** | Lateral border scapula | Greater tubercle |
| **Subscapularis** | Subscapular fossa | Lesser tubercle |

> All insert on humerus — scapula → humerus

## Other Important GH Muscles

| Muscle | Origin | Insertion |
|--------|--------|-----------|
| **Deltoid** | Clavicle + acromion + spine | Deltoid tuberosity |
| **Pectoralis Major** | Clavicle + sternum | Lateral lip of bicipital groove |
| **Latissimus Dorsi** | Thoracolumbar fascia | Floor of bicipital groove |
| **Teres Major** | Inferior angle scapula | Medial lip of groove |

> Everything inserts around the bicipital groove

## Elbow Flexors (Anterior)

| Muscle | Origin | Insertion |
|--------|--------|-----------|
| **Biceps Brachii** | Coracoid + supraglenoid | Radial tuberosity |
| **Brachialis** | Anterior humerus | Coronoid process |
| **Brachioradialis** | Lateral humerus | Distal radius |

## Elbow Extensors (Posterior)

| Muscle | Origin | Insertion |
|--------|--------|-----------|
| **Triceps Brachii** | Infraglenoid + humerus | Olecranon |

## Forearm Muscles — THE PATTERN

**🔥 Key Insight:**
- **Flexors** → originate from **MEDIAL EPICONDYLE**
- **Extensors** → originate from **LATERAL EPICONDYLE**

### Wrist Flexors (all from medial epicondyle)
- Flexor Carpi Radialis → base 2nd metacarpal
- Flexor Carpi Ulnaris → pisiform
- Palmaris Longus → palmar aponeurosis

### Wrist Extensors (all from lateral epicondyle)
- Extensor Carpi Radialis Longus → 2nd metacarpal
- Extensor Carpi Ulnaris → 5th metacarpal

## Hand — General Patterns

- **Thenar** (thumb): From carpals → thumb
- **Hypothenar** (pinky): From carpals → 5th digit
- **Lumbricals**: From tendons → extensor hood
- **Interossei**: Between metacarpals

# Capsular Pattern (GH Joint)

Order of restriction (most restricted → least restricted):

1. **External Rotation** (most restricted)
2. **Abduction**
3. **Internal Rotation** (least restricted)

Seen in: Adhesive capsulitis (frozen shoulder)

---

# How to Memorize Fast

🔑 **Patterns:**
- Rotator cuff → scapula → humerus
- Flexors → medial epicondyle
- Extensors → lateral epicondyle
- Big GH muscles → bicipital groove
- Elbow flexors → radius/ulna anterior
- Elbow extensors → olecranon
