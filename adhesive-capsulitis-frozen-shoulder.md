# Adhesive Capsulitis (Frozen Shoulder)

## What is Adhesive Capsulitis?

It's when the joint capsule of the shoulder becomes thick, tight, and "stuck", causing:
- Major loss of motion (ROM)
- Pain (especially early on)
- Stiffness in ALL directions (anterior most common)

**Key idea:** The joint can't glide or slide normally, so movement is very limited.

---

## The Main Problem (Condition)

### Tight / "Frozen" Joint Capsule

**What it is:** The capsule around the shoulder becomes:
- Thickened
- Inflamed
- Tight (adhesions form)

**Problem:**
- No normal joint gliding/sliding
- Loss of motion in ALL directions:
  - External rotation (MOST limited)
  - Abduction
  - Internal rotation
- Often worse in the anterior (front) capsule

---

## Stages of Adhesive Capsulitis (Important!)

### 1) Freezing Stage (Painful Stage)

**What's happening:**
- Increasing pain
- Gradual loss of motion

**Exercise focus:**
- Gentle, pain-free ROM
- Pendulum exercises
- Avoid aggressive stretching

### 2) Frozen Stage (Stiff Stage)

**What's happening:**
- Less pain
- Severe stiffness

**Exercise focus:**
- Stretching begins
- Joint mobilization
- Active + passive ROM

### 3) Thawing Stage (Recovery Stage)

**What's happening:**
- Motion slowly returns

**Exercise focus:**
- More aggressive stretching
- Strengthening
- Functional movement

---

## Exercises to Address the Problem

### Improve Mobility (MOST important)
- Pendulum swings
- Wand exercises (assistive ROM)
- Wall climbs (finger walks)

### Stretch the Capsule
- Cross-body stretch
- External rotation stretch
- Sleeper stretch

### Joint Mobilization (usually done by PT)
- Helps restore glide and slide

### Strengthening (later stages)
- Light rotator cuff strengthening
- Scapular stabilization

---

## Easy Way to Remember

**"Frozen shoulder = stuck capsule"**

- Early → pain → go gentle
- Middle → stiff → start stretching
- Late → loosening → strengthen

---

## Super Simple Summary

Adhesive capsulitis is when the shoulder capsule becomes tight and stuck, causing:
- Loss of motion in all directions
- Decreased joint gliding
- Most limited in external rotation

**Treatment depends on stage:**
- Early: gentle movement
- Middle: stretching
- Late: strengthening
