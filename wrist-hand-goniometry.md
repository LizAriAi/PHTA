# Wrist & Hand Goniometry

## Wrist Flexion
- **Patient Position:** Sitting
- **Axis:** Ulnar Styloid
- **Proximal/Stationary Arm:** Lateral Epicondyle
- **Distal/Moving Arm:** 5th Metacarpal
- **Normal ROM:** 68° - 80°

## Wrist Extension
- **Patient Position:** Sitting
- **Axis:** Ulnar Styloid
- **Proximal/Stationary Arm:** Lateral Epicondyle
- **Distal/Moving Arm:** 5th Metacarpal
- **Normal ROM:** 60° - 75°

## Radial Deviation
- **Patient Position:** Sitting
- **Axis:** Capitate (bottom of 3rd metacarpal, "in the hole")
- **Proximal/Stationary Arm:** Midline of Forearm
- **Distal/Moving Arm:** 3rd Metacarpal
- **Normal ROM:** 20° - 25°

## Ulnar Deviation
- **Patient Position:** Sitting
- **Axis:** Capitate (bottom of 3rd metacarpal, "in the hole")
- **Proximal/Stationary Arm:** Midline of Forearm
- **Distal/Moving Arm:** 3rd Metacarpal
- **Normal ROM:** 30° - 40°

## Finger Flexion

### MCP (Metacarpophalangeal) Flexion
- **Patient Position:** Sitting
- **Axis:** MCP Joint (lateral/medial aspect)
- **Proximal/Stationary Arm:** Metacarpal
- **Distal/Moving Arm:** Proximal Phalanx
- **Normal ROM:** 90° - 100°

### PIP (Proximal Interphalangeal) Flexion
- **Patient Position:** Sitting
- **Axis:** PIP Joint (lateral/medial aspect)
- **Proximal/Stationary Arm:** Proximal Phalanx
- **Distal/Moving Arm:** Middle Phalanx
- **Normal ROM:** 100° - 110°

### DIP (Distal Interphalangeal) Flexion
- **Patient Position:** Sitting
- **Axis:** DIP Joint (lateral/medial aspect)
- **Proximal/Stationary Arm:** Middle Phalanx
- **Distal/Moving Arm:** Distal Phalanx
- **Normal ROM:** 70° - 90°

## MCP Adduction & Abduction

### Abduction
- **Patient Position:** Sitting
- **Axis:** MCP Joint (dorsal aspect)
- **Proximal/Stationary Arm:** Metacarpal
- **Distal/Moving Arm:** Proximal Phalanx
- **Normal ROM:** 25°

### Adduction
- **Patient Position:** Sitting
- **Axis:** MCP Joint (dorsal aspect)
- **Proximal/Stationary Arm:** Metacarpal
- **Distal/Moving Arm:** Proximal Phalanx
- **Normal ROM:** Return to neutral (not typically measured)

---

## Opposition
- **Patient Position:** Sitting
- **Measurement:** Tip of thumb to tip of 5th finger
- **Normal:** Should be able to touch
- **Note:** Look for decreasing distance between fingertips over time. No normal value.
