# Wrist & Hand Goniometry

## Wrist Flexion
- **Patient Position:** Sitting
- **Axis:** Ulnar Styloid
- **Proximal/Stationary Arm:** Lateral Epicondyle
- **Distal/Moving Arm:** 5th Metacarpal
- **Normal ROM:** 68° - 80°

## Wrist Extension
- **Patient Position:** Sitting
- **Axis:** Ulnar Styloid
- **Proximal/Stationary Arm:** Lateral Epicondyle
- **Distal/Moving Arm:** 5th Metacarpal
- **Normal ROM:** 60° - 75°

## Radial Deviation
- **Patient Position:** Sitting
- **Axis:** Capitate (bottom of 3rd metacarpal, "in the hole")
- **Proximal/Stationary Arm:** Midline of Forearm
- **Distal/Moving Arm:** 3rd Metacarpal
- **Normal ROM:** 20° - 25°

## Ulnar Deviation
- **Patient Position:** Sitting
- **Axis:** Capitate (bottom of 3rd metacarpal, "in the hole")
- **Proximal/Stationary Arm:** Midline of Forearm
- **Distal/Moving Arm:** 3rd Metacarpal
- **Normal ROM:** 30° - 40°
