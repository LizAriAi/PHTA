# Wrist & Hand Manual Muscle Testing (MMT)

## Wrist Flexion
- **Patient Position:** Sitting, palm down, arm off table
- **Resistance:** On metacarpals (back of hand)
- **Stabilization:** Forearm
- **Gravity Minimized Position:** Bring elbow back, place hand on table (karate chop style)

## Wrist Extension
- **Patient Position:** Sitting, palm face up
- **Resistance:** On metacarpals (on palm)
- **Stabilization:** Forearm, just by the wrist
- **Gravity Minimized Position:** Bring elbow back, place hand on table (karate chop style)
